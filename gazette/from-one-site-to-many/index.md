# From one site to many

After [connecting Field Notes to its workbook](https://rawthoughts.in/gazette/connect-publications/), you have two publications with a useful path between them. The same arrangement can connect a personal site, a research publication and product documentation. Each keeps its own readers, writing and appearance.

<a id="keep-the-four-jobs-clear"></a>

## Keep the four jobs clear

Field Notes puts a small example of each job in one folder. A larger workspace can select them from separate places:

**Writing.** Field Notes keeps articles and their resources in `content/`. In a larger workspace, the writing stays in the owning project's Content folder, with its Notes, figures and other resources. One publication can select several project sources.

**Configuration.** Field Notes uses `gazette.toml` to choose its sources, address, theme and outputs. One larger workspace makes those choices for the publications you are reading here.

**Compilation.** The same Gazette executable resolves the pages and their relationships, then supplies HTML, navigation and discovery outputs.

**Design.** Field Notes has a small theme of its own, `theme:field-notes`. A larger publication can use shared reading and navigation behaviour while its world supplies the visual language. These publications share Base through their world themes.

For example, Pale's product guides stay with Pale, but readers encounter them at [RawThoughts' Pale entrance](https://rawthoughts.in/pale/). A standalone Pale essay belongs to RawThoughts' Blog. The source owner, the article's place in the publication and its address answer different questions; selecting the source supplies no reason to duplicate it.

<a id="follow-an-idea-across-publications"></a>

## Follow an idea across publications

[How Pale fits together](https://rawthoughts.in/collections/systems/making-a-working-world-intelligible/architecture/) explains why a working system needs a sufficiently stable description of the work it runs. It then leads to [MONAD](https://causality.in/monad/), which asks what a principled change of a description would need to preserve. The crossing has a reason: the second publication develops the research question raised by the first.

RawThoughts uses Carrier; Causality uses Lattice. Their shared Base theme can present the destination's visual language at the crossing, while each page keeps its own publication's composition. Gazette supplies the destination information; the theme decides how to show it. Field Notes' workbook exercise establishes the link and address behaviour, but its starter theme does not reproduce those world treatments.

The same workspace also connects [getHarsh](https://getharsh.in/), [Post-AGI Economics](https://post-agi-economics.org/), [Sleepwalker](https://sleepwalker.in/) and [DAO Studio](https://daostudio.in/). Their different purposes remain visible even when an idea gives readers a reason to move between them.

<a id="expand-the-workspace-when-the-writing-needs-it"></a>

## Expand the workspace when the writing needs it

Start by deciding who the next publication is for and which writing belongs there. Select its sources, publisher, address and theme, then add the references that help readers continue a thought. A related subject can need a collection within the existing site; it does not automatically need another domain.

Preview both sides of each crossing and follow the return path. Local addresses belong to that preview session; keep the publication references in the articles. Before delivery, [review the selected pages with their publisher](https://rawthoughts.in/gazette/review-and-deliver/). Sharing a compiler or theme leaves each publisher's approval separate.

Gazette builds these publications on its own, from the sources you selected. [Pale](https://rawthoughts.in/pale/understand-your-world/#use-connected-applications-for-their-own-strengths) asking for a build through Gazette's own interface for a broader task, and permitted Weave records among the sources, are the two connections in front. Gazette needs no live call to Scope, Counsel or Reckon to turn those captured inputs into a publication.
