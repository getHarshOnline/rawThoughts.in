Questions about the products

How can I try the products?

Start with a task: give coding agents shared repository instructions, build a publication or work through a RawFish rehearsal. The first two run from a local source build, and each guide says what you need before you begin. RawFish's guide reads without installing anything, because the retained prototype covers only part of the rehearsal it describes.

What question do these products answer?

How does a person or an organisation form a working picture of its work, act through it, learn from what follows and stay accountable as agents do more of the work? Each product answers a part of it, and each can be used alone except Scope, which brings its context from Pale, Weave or both. Weave keeps the records, Scope brings the context a task needs, Counsel judges where rules leave a choice, RawFish rehearses a direction before anyone commits to it, Gazette publishes a body of thought, and Pale is the meta harness through which they act, a harness over the tools people already use.

How do these products fit together?

Together the products form one system a person runs, with people deciding: the records hold the world, something is observed, a person decides what to do about it, a direction can be tried before anyone commits to it, the act goes out, and its outcome returns as a record that shapes what is learned next. What joins them is one problem: agents decide far more often than people, so by the time an agent writes, what it read has usually changed. A read says what else to read, and a write is tried for what it affects before it goes in; the Systems collection follows that through the parts. Pale's local tools, Gazette and Reckon’s laboratory run today and the other parts join one at a time; each product's page says what runs and the problem in front of it.

In the integrated system, requests for Scope’s context, Counsel’s judgment and Reckon’s evaluation pass through Pale. Scope always works with Pale, Weave or both: Weave keeps the records and points to the files around them, Pale keeps the history of what each piece of work relied on and what followed, and Scope combines the two into one account an agent can ask for what it needs. Without Pale, Scope is how an agent reads Weave. Harsh describes three sides that keep the system improving safely: the recursive self-improvement side, where Microsoft's SkillOpt, Reckon and Counsel are joined by Pale's genealogy, its history of what each piece of work relied on and what followed, and improve the methods between runs; the continual-learning side, where a person's workspace and Pale's SDKs, language server, VS Code extension and MCP let a person and their agent keep rewriting the description of their world; and the context engineering and alignment side, where Scope, that genealogy and Weave give each act the context it needs and keep it within bounds. Gazette builds publications from the sources you select and does not call Scope, Counsel or Reckon; supplying it an organisation's permitted Weave records is the connection in front. RawFish rehearses a decision through a simulated world. Reckon, under Causality, supplies the separate evaluation instrument. Pale’s introduction explains how those contributions fit without moving their records or decisions into one application.

Weave alone is a headless Jira and Salesforce, a database with a capable interface, and that is an intended way to use it. Pale alone is a hook runtime and context declaration engine that asks you to tune your files and knowledge closely, an orchestrator of agent sandboxes and a permission system for agents. Together they give something more: people and agents working inside one organisation and across several, each instance kept separate, reads and writes delegated across instances, and a structured memory for people and agents, serving the same headless Jira and Salesforce to both.

Where should I start reading?

Open a product’s guides to understand it or try a supported workflow. Use the developer docs at docs.rawthoughts.in to build the product, integrate with its code or extend it.
