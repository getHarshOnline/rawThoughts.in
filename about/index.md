An idea becomes clearer when someone has to use it.

RawThoughts brings together software, consulting and writing around a practical question: what would make this idea useful in the world? That can mean composing intelligence around a person’s work, making a business process reliable or finding a better way to describe the problem itself. We use the work to test what a team needs to remember when a decision or responsibility passes to someone else.

The name began with [Harsh Joshi’s](https://getharsh.in/about/) (on getHarsh) blogging and exploration and became his working identity in March 2020. Since 2023, it has also been the consulting and enterprise face of RawThoughts Enterprises Private Limited (RTEPL). “Without filters” means a question can be examined before it fits a product category or a polished thesis; it still needs judgement.

[Radio became Spotify](https://rawthoughts.in/blog/radio-became-spotify/) is the lens: a human need persists while the way of meeting it changes the business, its measures and our habits. We work with business owners making their own transition, following what AI can change from the customer’s request through the organisation’s response.

## A collaboration

Bring one valuable workflow into focus, find what prevents the result and make the agreed change. Consulting connects product judgment and hands-on engineering to an outcome the team can recognise.

[Consider an engagement](https://rawthoughts.in/services/)

The work changes the question. The question changes what gets built.

A model’s answer becomes useful through the decisions and actions it makes possible. Understanding the customer’s day, the team’s responsibilities and the systems already in use can change the right intervention. Sometimes that calls for new software; sometimes it reveals a simpler change to what is already there.

Building and inquiry keep informing one another. An operational difficulty can expose a research question; a better explanation can make a smaller, more useful product possible.

More capable systems change the work and the questions worth asking.

RawThoughts itself is the applied work: the systems we build and the consulting we do. It carries beliefs about what AI changes in how organisations work and in the economy around them, and tests them against real problems. Two inquiries grow from those beliefs, each with its own publication, methods and questions. [Post-AGI Economics](https://post-agi-economics.org/about/) (on Post-AGI Economics) follows cheaper machine agency into the economy: what becomes scarce, who owns it and how the gains reach households. [Causality](https://causality.in/about/) (on Causality) asks what intelligence needs to represent and how its organisation can learn. Practical work gives them new problems to examine, and their ideas can change the next system we build.

RawThoughts is RTEPL’s consulting and publishing brand. [Harsh Joshi](https://getharsh.in/about/) (on getHarsh) is its Principal Consultant and the author of this work.
