Make work flow across teams and systems

Your AI pilot gives good answers, and nobody on the team can say what it actually did or what happens when it's wrong. Most of the time that's not the model being stupid. It's the process around it.

We take on one expensive workflow at a time, through a paid audit, then a build sprint, then a retainer if it earns one. Pale can also be set up for one team in your organisation, shaped around that team's people, tools and permissions.

  1. Paid audit

Before we work together

Is this right for our business?

It fits recurring work whose delays, rework or failures cost the business. Bring a sponsor who can approve change and an operating owner who knows the daily work. We start with one workflow and a result worth improving.

What should we send you to get started?

Send a recent example: what should happen, what happens instead and what it costs your team or customers. Include who owns the workflow and the result you want to improve. No technical specification needed.

Does the audit commit us to a build?

No. You receive a diagnosis and recommendation as a separate paid engagement. If a sprint is worthwhile, we agree its scope and acceptance conditions before building.

When is a retainer useful?

When changing requirements, growing use or recurring problems need continuing product and architecture work. It is optional, with a scope agreed around that work.

Do we need AI or new software?

Often the useful change is a better process, configuration or connection between existing tools. We build software or use AI where it closes a real gap and improves the result.

How will we know it worked?

Agree the baseline and measures before building: perhaps fewer repeat visits, less rework or a shorter gap between completed work and billing. Test those measures across routine work and failure cases.

Can we ask about one of your products?

Yes. Tell us which product interests you and what you need it to do, and we shape the engagement around your work. A Pale pilot starts with one team. DAO Studio's products are also available through RawThoughts consulting, with scope, integration, deployment and licensing agreed for each engagement. DAO Studio retains its product ownership.

When is this a poor fit?

When nobody can own the workflow, provide access to the people doing it or approve change. “Add AI” also needs a problem behind it. We need one useful result we can agree and test together.

Who will we work with?

Harsh Joshi, Principal Consultant at RawThoughts, leads the product, architecture and hands-on engineering work alongside your operating and technical owners.
