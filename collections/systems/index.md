Title: Systems

[Read HTML](https://rawthoughts.in/collections/systems/)

Together [the products](https://rawthoughts.in/products/) form one system a person runs, with people deciding: the records hold the world, something is observed, a person decides what to do about it, a direction can be tried before anyone commits to it, the act goes out, and its outcome returns as a record that shapes what is learned next. The journeys here and RawFish's collection take up four of its parts as problems of their own; the records [Weave](https://rawthoughts.in/weave/) keeps and the publication [Gazette](https://rawthoughts.in/gazette/) sends out are introduced on their own pages.

Agents decide far more often than people, so by the time one writes, what it read has usually changed. That is the problem the products share: a read has to say what else needs reading, and a change has to be tried for what it affects before it is made.

A product changes, and an essay describing it needs another look. Its user guide may still be accurate. A research article may explain an earlier experiment whose result has not changed at all. Knowing that all three mention the product does not tell you which claims need revision. You need the particular relationship between each account and the work it describes.

Making those relationships explicit can save repeated reconstruction. It also creates something to maintain. A useful system must preserve the differences that matter: an earlier account and a current premise, a recommendation and the person's decision, a result supplied and a result accepted. Its parts need to exchange enough context to cooperate while keeping responsibility for their own work.

These journeys follow that design problem into the parts and the comparisons between them. The architecture of [Pale](https://rawthoughts.in/pale/) needs a reason to exist; the context from [Scope](https://rawthoughts.in/scope/) needs to help the task; the judgment of [Counsel](https://rawthoughts.in/counsel/) needs something better to learn from than agreement with its own answer. Rehearsing a decision in [RawFish](https://rawthoughts.in/rawfish/) adds another way to try a direction before anyone decides: an imagined objection can expose a question to investigate with the people affected. The test throughout is whether the structure makes the work easier to understand and continue, including when something changes or fails.
