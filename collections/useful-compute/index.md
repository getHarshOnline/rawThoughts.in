Title: Useful compute

[Read HTML](https://rawthoughts.in/collections/useful-compute/)

Author: Harsh Joshi

A cheaper GPU hour can be the dearer way to finish an experiment. The rate looks attractive until data preparation consumes the reservation, communication slows every step or an interruption leaves the result trapped on the rented machine. The purchase makes sense around the work it can carry to completion, within the interval in which that result matters.

That connects procurement to engineering. [Job cost](https://rawthoughts.in/collections/useful-compute/a-gpu-price-is-not-a-job-cost/) depends on what has to run and survive, while [the capacity clock](https://rawthoughts.in/collections/useful-compute/a-capacity-promise-needs-a-clock/) determines whether the experiment can happen at all. A fitting model still needs an [execution arrangement](https://rawthoughts.in/collections/useful-compute/making-the-cluster-useful/) that can move its data, coordinate its workers and retain progress. Price, time and configuration are parts of the same choice.

The people on either side of that purchase face different pressures. A supplier pays for [capacity between jobs](https://rawthoughts.in/blog/the-cost-of-an-idle-fleet/); a buyer pays to reach an acceptable result; someone using [an interactive service](https://rawthoughts.in/blog/serving-tokens-is-a-service-problem/) pays in waiting too. Raising one utilisation number can help one of them while making another's experience worse. The interesting design is an arrangement that reduces the real burden without hiding who now carries it.

Following that arrangement beyond the machine opens a [larger economic question](https://getharsh.in/blog/from-compute-to-an-economic-question/). A [usable service](https://rawthoughts.in/blog/the-cluster-is-only-the-beginning/) makes the experiment easier to carry out. [Useful national infrastructure](https://rawthoughts.in/collections/ideas/india-ai/testing-indias-ai-crossing/from-posters-to-throughput/) makes more such work possible. What happens to the resulting gain, and who can afford the output, leads into [Post-AGI Economics](https://post-agi-economics.org/).
